# Materials and surface state

RAVEN separates **material identity** from **surface state**.

Material identity answers what a surface is: concrete, painted steel, denim, skin, rubber, wet soil. It carries physical traits and a layered procedural material graph.

Surface state answers what has happened to it: wetness, mud, dust, blood, burn and damage. State may change every frame without recompiling the underlying material.

This lets rain, footsteps, bullets, ecology and VFX modify a shared representation instead of each system inventing its own decals and roughness hacks.
